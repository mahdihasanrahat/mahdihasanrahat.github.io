About Me

Engineering with purpose.

I am a third-year Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of electrical engineering and artificial intelligence. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent computational methods for modern power systems.

My current research focuses on machine learning and deep learning approaches for power-system applications, including grid inertia forecasting, probabilistic prediction, physics-informed learning, and risk assessment.

I am continuously developing my skills in Python, MATLAB, Simulink, machine learning, deep learning, and power-system analysis while building a foundation for future graduate research.
